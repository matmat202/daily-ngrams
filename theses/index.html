      Titles per 1,000 that contain the word, out of every Stanford thesis that period (about 62,000 in all, from SearchWorks). A word also counts its plural, so network finds networks; neur* finds any word starting neur; a phrase matches as typed. Only titles are searched, not abstracts. Early periods hold few theses, so a single title moves their line a lot: the tooltip gives the count. Hover or tap a period to read the titles.
